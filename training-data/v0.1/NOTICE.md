# Data sources and their licences (roman2khmer v0.1 training data and model files)

The code is MIT (`LICENSE`). The model files (`translit.npz`, `reverse.npz`, `khmer_bigram.npz`) are released under
**CC BY-SA 4.0**, because part of the training data comes from Khmer Wikipedia (CC BY-SA). They were trained or
counted from:

| Source | Used for | Licence and notice |
|---|---|---|
| Chhunneng, *khmer-text-transliteration* (github.com/Chhunneng/khmer-text-transliteration, commit fe8028f1) | word pairs (romanised ↔ Khmer) | MIT, © 2025 Chrea Chanchhunneng (notice below). The origin of the pairs is not documented by the author. |
| Khmer Wikipedia, dump `kmwiki-20261001-pages-articles.xml.bz2` (https://dumps.wikimedia.org/kmwiki/20261001/) | 3,532 name and loanword pairs mined automatically from bracketed Latin spellings in article text, then filtered (length, script, held-out data) | CC BY-SA 4.0, © Khmer Wikipedia contributors (article histories at https://km.wikipedia.org). |
| FineWeb-2, subset `khm_Khmr`, file `train_000_00000.parquet` (https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-2) | 63,805 mined pairs (filtered as above); and `khmer_bigram.npz`: word and word-pair counts from 200,000 documents (text split at punctuation, Khmer runs split into words by greedy longest match against the word list, pairs seen fewer than 2 times dropped; 92,905 words, 2.5M pairs) | ODC-By 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/); FineWeb-2 is also subject to Common Crawl's terms of use (https://commoncrawl.org/terms-of-use). We regard the count tables as a Produced Work of the FineWeb-2 database and attribute it here; ODC-By covers database rights, not the underlying web pages. |
| Google `language-resources`, Khmer lexicon `km/data/lexicon.tsv` (commit bc72dd65) | pronunciation side task during training (not shipped); word list for the word-pair statistics | CC BY 4.0, © 2018 Google Inc. |
| Gemini, `gemini-3.6-flash` through the Gemini API (free tier), 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-06 | romanised spellings of common Khmer words, filtered by fixed rules | Generated output under the Gemini API Additional Terms (version effective 2026-03-23). The author's reading: a narrow transliterator does not compete with the service; this is an interpretation, not a clearance. |
| GPT, `gpt-6.1-sol` through the Codex CLI with a ChatGPT login, 2026-11-06 | same | Generated output under OpenAI's terms of use for that service; same interpretation and caveat. |
| The author's own typing and checked spellings | word pairs | the author's |

**Excluded on purpose:** spellings generated only by Claude (Anthropic) and LLM drafts whose generating model was not
recorded. Spellings that Gemini or GPT also produced are kept. Claude-written chat messages were used to choose three
decoding settings (alpha, beta, gamma) and to evaluate; they are not training data. This exclusion is the author's
conservative choice, not a stated legal requirement. Spellings kept because Gemini or GPT also gave them were
generated by those services in their own calls.
